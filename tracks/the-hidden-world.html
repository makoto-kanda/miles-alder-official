Liner Notes

What if an ordinary sidewalk held a secret that most people would walk right past? “The Hidden World” begins with that simple idea and follows the discovery of a world hiding in plain sight.

The song is inspired by geocaching, an outdoor treasure-hunting activity in which people use GPS coordinates to find containers hidden by others. Some are tucked into parks, built into everyday surroundings, or concealed in places that reveal themselves only to someone who knows where to look. Finders sign a logbook, return the container to its hiding place, and leave the discovery for the next person.

But the real discovery in this song is bigger than finding a hidden container. It's the moment when a familiar place becomes unfamiliar in the best possible way — when a small piece of paper connects you to strangers who have stood in exactly the same spot, and the world suddenly seems full of possibilities you never noticed before.

Sometimes, all it takes is looking a little closer.
