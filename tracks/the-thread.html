Liner Notes

Sometimes, after following the same trail of ideas for a while, one small connection finally brings everything into focus. “The Thread” looks back on a puzzle-solving session from years ago, when a group of friends chased one idea after another—and, for once, the narrator found a clue that moved things forward.

There's no claim of brilliance here, just the quiet surprise of seeing a way through when others haven't yet found it. For a moment, the usual pattern shifts, and the person who has been trying to keep up becomes the one worth following. Sometimes, all it takes is finding the right thread—and being a little lucky.
