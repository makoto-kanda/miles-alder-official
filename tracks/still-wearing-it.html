Liner Notes

Sometimes, the most meaningful gifts are the ones that quietly become part of everyday life. What begins as someone else's idea gradually turns into a habit of your own — not through sudden transformation, but through small reminders that make it a little harder to ignore what you already know.

“Still Wearing It” is about that imperfect process. There are excuses, creative arguments, occasional victories, and plenty of evidence that doesn't quite support the case being made. Progress comes slowly, and not every number tells a flattering story. But somewhere between the data and the daily routine, something starts to change.

It's also a song about the person behind the gift: someone whose encouragement stays present even across a few thousand miles. The goal isn't perfection. It's simply to keep showing up, keep learning, and keep trying — one day at a time.
